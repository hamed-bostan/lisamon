About the Artist

MAEDEH AKBARI FARIMANI

Maedeh Akbari Farimani is a Dubai-based independent visual artist with a background in painting and sculpture.

Holding a Bachelor of Arts in Painting, her artistic practice explores the human form, emotions, identity, and the relationship between material and expression.

Working across painting, sculpture, and mixed media, she experiments with texture, dimension, and unconventional forms to create thought-provoking works that move beyond traditional artistic boundaries.

Her work reflects a personal exploration of the human experience, balancing abstraction, symbolism, and contemporary expression.
